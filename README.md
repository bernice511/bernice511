## 🚀 Featured Experience: PRINCE @ Thoughtworks for Bayer
**PRINCE (Preclinical Information Centre)** is a supervisor-orchestrated multi-agent knowledge engine used in Bayer's daily preclinical drug-safety research. I worked on it from 2022 to 2025, first on its data platform and then leading development of the GenAI system.
- 🏆 **Bayer GenAI Award for Best Technical Implementation** (2025)
- 🔎 **Hybrid RAG** over Amazon OpenSearch with query expansion and bge-reranker re-ranking, on 100 GB+ of biomedical data
- 🧮 **Text-to-SQL** with dynamic few-shot prompting against AWS Athena: **90%+ accuracy**
- 🏷️ **NER metadata extraction** from study reports: **98%+ accuracy across 40+ fields**
- ✅ **Eval as a CI gate**: RAGAS + DeepEval on every change, with Langfuse tracing
- 📈 **Impact**: ad-hoc data requests went from days to minutes, and complex queries got 30% faster
